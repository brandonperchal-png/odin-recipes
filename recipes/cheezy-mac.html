Cheezy Mac

Posted February 24, 2025

About

When I first moved to New York in 2015, I became obessed with trying a variety of mac n'cheese dishes from different restraurants. My top favorite go-tos were The Smith and S'Mac, and this recipe is homeage to the delicious mac n'cheese dishes that I tried. While this recipe is dairy free, it still slaps like a classic mac n'cheese! It's extremely creamy, cheezy, and each bite brings more and more comfort than the first. This has become an instant classic and go to for an easy dinner. I hope you enjoy this as much as I do!

Time

  • Prep Time: 15 minutes
  • Cook Time: 10 - 15 minutes
  • Total Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients

Yields 6 servings
  • 1 lbs of elbow macoroni
  • 2 large Russet potatoes, rough chopped
  • 1 large carrot, rough chopped
  • 1/2 cup of raw cashews
  • 1/4 of red bell pepper, rough chopped
  • 1/2 white onion, rough chopped
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 cup of nutriional yest
  • 2 tablespoons of Dijion Mustard
  • 1 teaspoon of apple cider vinegar
  • Salt and peper to taste

Optional Garnish

  • Lightly toasted breadcrumbs

Directions

  1. Cook the elbow macoroni according to package instructions in salted water. Save 1 cup of the pasta water before draining and setting aside.
  2. In a large pot, add the rough chopped potatoes, carrots, red bell pepper, onion, garlic, and cashews. Cover with water and bring to a boil. Cook until the potatoes are falling apart with a fork, about 10 - 15 mins.
  3. Drain the cooked veggies and cashews, and transfer them to a blender with the 1 cup of pasta water, nutritional yeast, dijon mustard, and apple cider vinegar. Blend until smooth and creamy. Add salt and pepper to taste. Tip: Actual cheese is very salty, so don't be afraid add more salt than you think you need.
  4. Back in the large pot, combine the cooked macoroni and cheezy sauce. Toss until the macoroni is evenly coated with the sauce.
  5. Transfer the cheezy mac to a serving dish, and top with the optional toasted breadcrumbs. Serve warm and enjoy!

Recipe is based on a vegan mac n'cheeze recipe that I found in a cookbook.
