Garden Focaccia

Posted May 15, 2024

About

This delicous garden focaccia has quickly become my most requested bread to bring over to friends, and the fun secret is all you need is yeast, patience, and a lot of olive oil to make this bread taste like a delieous sourdough focaccia. It's one of the first breads that I made, and I hope this recipe gives you the confidence to make your own delicious fococcia.

Time

  • Prep Time: 30 minutes
  • Bake Time: 20 to 25 minutes
  • Rest Time: 24 to 48 hours
  • Total Time: 48 hours and 55 mins

Ingredients

Yields 1 13" x 18" focaccia

Dough

  • 3 cups (360g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 (9g) teaspoons table salt
  • 1 teaspoon (5g) granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon (3g) active dry yeast
  • 1 1/4 cups (284g) warm water (90°F to 110°F)
  • 1 1/2 tablespoons (18g) extra virgin olive oil, plus more for coating the bowl

Pan and Topping

  • 2 tablespoons (26g) extra virgin olive oil, plus more for coatiing the pan
  • Herbs and veggies of your choice.

Directions

  1. Weigh your flour; or measure it by gently spooning it into a cup, then sweeping off any excess. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, salt, sugar, and yeast.
  2. Add the warm water and olive oil to the dry ingredients. Mix until a shaggy dough forms. Knead the dough for 8 to 10 minutes, until it is smooth and elastic. You can knead by hand or use a stand mixer with a dough hook attachment.
  3. In a seperate large bowl, coat the insides with extra virgin olive oil. Place the dough in the bowl, cover it with plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm place for 12 - 24 hours. The dough should be doubled in size.
  4. After the dough has risen, gently deflate it and fold the dough like a book in the bowl. Once folded, transfer it to a well-oiled 13" x 18" baking pan. Stretch and press the dough evenly into the pan. Cover the dough with plastic wrap and let it rise for another 12 hours in the refrigerator.
  5. Preheat the oven to 475°F (246°C). Remove the dough from the refrigerator and let it come to room temperature for about 30 minutes. Use your fingers to dimple the dough, creating small dimples all over the surface about 1 1/2" apart. The goal is to throughly dimple the dough without deflating it or pushing all the way through the dough. Drizzle the top with olive oil and sprinkle with your choice of herbs and veggies.
  6. Bake the focaccia in the preheated oven for 20 to 25 minutes, or until it is golden brown and sounds hollow when tapped. Remove from the oven and let it cool in the pan for a few minutes before transferring it to a wire rack to cool completely.
  7. Slice and serve the focaccia warm or at room temperature. Enjoy!

Recipe is based on a recipe from King Arthur Baking.
